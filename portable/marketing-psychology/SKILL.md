# Marketing Psychology & Mental Models

Expert application of psychological principles and mental models to marketing. Helps identify why people buy, how to influence behavior ethically, and how to make better marketing decisions.

## Use when

- Designing campaigns and need to understand audience decision-making.
- Improving conversion rates, messaging, or pricing strategy.
- Applying specific cognitive biases (loss aversion, social proof, scarcity) to copy.
- Building product-market fit frameworks grounded in behavioral science.
- User mentions: psychology, mental models, cognitive bias, persuasion, behavioral science, why people buy, decision-making, consumer behavior.

## Do not use when

- Task is purely technical implementation with no behavioral/messaging component.

## How to apply

1. Identify which category applies: **foundational strategy**, **buyer psychology**, **persuasion & influence**, **pricing & value**, **content & engagement**, **trust & authority**, or **growth & retention**.
2. Select 1–3 models most relevant to the situation (avoid model soup).
3. Explain the psychology behind the model.
4. Provide specific, ethical marketing application.
5. Suggest how to test/measure the effect.

## Product marketing context

If `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` exists, read it before applying models. Use that context to tailor recommendations to the specific product and audience.
