# GrenadianBuzz Newsletter Templates

**Dedicated template pack for diaspora-focused newsletters**

A collection of production-ready, reusable email templates designed for Grenadian diaspora audiences. Each template is structured for clarity, personalization, and engagement.

## Template Overview

### 1. Weekly Roundup (`weekly-roundup.md`)

**Best for**: Core diaspora newsletter with mix of news, culture, and engagement

**Structure** (1,465 words typical):
- Hero/opener (75 words)
- Top story (350 words)
- "In Brief" section (5 shorter stories, ~500 words)
- Cultural spotlight (175 words, rotating theme)
- Events this week (150 words)
- Community spotlight (175 words)
- Radio recommendations (40 words, optional)
- Footer with preferences

**Personalization options**:
- Premium subscriber bonuses (exclusive content, early event access)
- Family tier highlights (kids corner, family events)
- Geographic segmentation (N. America focus, UK focus, Caribbean)

**Curate time**: 3-4 hours  
**Send time**: Sundays, 9 AM EST (optimal for diaspora across zones)

**Key sections**:
- Top story: High-impact diaspora angle
- In brief: Mix of news, business, health, education, sports
- Cultural spotlight: Rotating weekly themes (artist, history, tradition)
- Events: Timezone-aware listings across diaspora cities
- Community: Grassroots initiatives and diaspora highlights

---

### 2. Breaking News Alert (`breaking-news-alert.md`)

**Best for**: Real-time, urgent notifications for major events

**Structure** (300-400 words typical):
- Alert banner (visual urgency)
- Headline & lead (inverted pyramid)
- Current status box (real-time updates)
- Main story (200-300 words)
- "What you need to know" (actionable bullets)
- How to help (if applicable)
- Live updates section
- Background/previous context (if multi-day)
- Footer with alert management

**Event categories**:
- Natural disasters (hurricanes, flooding, earthquakes)
- Government/political (elections, policy changes)
- Health emergencies (outbreaks, accidents)
- Sports/cultural (major wins, cancellations)
- Economic (layoffs, business launches, currency changes)

**Send protocol**:
- Critical events: Send immediately, update every 30 min
- Major events: Send immediately, update every 1-2 hrs
- Significant events: Send immediately, update every 2-4 hrs

**Key sections**:
- Alert banner: Eye-catching visual urgency
- Current status box: Real-time summary (status, severity, affected areas, impact)
- Main story: Context, timeline, current action, diaspora impact
- Action items: What to do if in Grenada, diaspora, or has flights booked
- How to help: Vetted charities, official channels, diaspora fundraisers
- Emergency contacts: Verified phone numbers and resources

---

### 3. Events & Culture Digest (`events-culture-digest.md`)

**Best for**: Celebrating festivals, cultural observances, diaspora gatherings

**Structure** (2,500+ words typical):
- Theme introduction (100-150 words)
- Featured event (400-500 words, high-profile)
- Upcoming events calendar (10-15 events, mixed island/diaspora)
- Cultural deep dive (200-250 words, rotating focus)
- Diaspora tradition spotlight (150-200 words)
- How to participate box (quick reference)
- Resource library (links, downloadables, organizing info)
- Footer with engagement options

**Monthly theme rotation**:
- Jan: New Year diaspora gatherings
- Feb: Carnival preparation
- Mar: Carnival peak + Independence Day
- Apr: Easter & spring observances
- May: Summer festivals & family reunions
- Jun: Emancipation Day
- Jul: Summer travel & diaspora road trips
- Aug: Heritage & vacation season
- Sep: Back-to-school cultural programs
- Oct: Preparedness & resilience
- Nov: Thanksgiving & harvest
- Dec: Holiday celebrations

**Key sections**:
- Featured event: Detailed description, participation options (travel, virtual, local), pricing, registration
- Event calendar: Mixed (10-15 events) including island events, diaspora watch parties, online classes, cultural observances
- Cultural deep-dive: Rotating topics (history, music/dance, traditions, culinary)
- Diaspora tradition spotlight: How diaspora celebrates/preserves culture
- How to participate: Perform, attend, celebrate locally, share
- Resource library: Planning guides, music/dance tutorials, cultural organizations, travel logistics

---

### 4. Community Spotlight (`community-spotlight.md`)

**Best for**: Celebrating diaspora changemakers, fundraisers, grassroots initiatives

**Structure** (1,800+ words typical):
- Opening/theme (75-100 words)
- Featured profile (400-500 words, Q&A or narrative)
- How to support (donations, volunteering, spreading word)
- Secondary spotlights (2-3 profiles, 150-200 words each)
- Nominate a changemaker (engagement driver)
- Community news roundup (5-8 brief updates)
- Community directory (organizations by region + topic)
- Footer with engagement & social

**Featured profile structure**:
- Name, location, photo
- Hook (why we're featuring them)
- Q&A or narrative (300-400 words)
- Impact summary
- How to support (multiple options)
- Social media + contact info

**Story types & rotation**:
- Featured profile: One person/initiative deep dive
- Secondary spotlights: 2-3 shorter features (mentorship, cultural preservation, mutual aid, business)
- Community news: Quick wins, announcements, milestones
- Directory: Curated list of organizations (updated bi-weekly)

**Key sections**:
- Featured profile: In-depth Q&A or narrative with diaspora member/initiative
- How to support: Donate, volunteer, spread word, participate
- Secondary spotlights: 2-3 shorter stories representing diverse initiatives
- Community news: 5-8 brief updates (scholarships, organizations, fundraisers, events)
- Nominate form: Simple 2-3 question form to surface new stories
- Organization directory: Regional + topic-based (North America, UK/Europe, Caribbean neighbors)

## Content Strategy Across All Templates

### Word Count Goals

| Template | Hero | Main Content | Sidebars | Total | Read Time |
|----------|------|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly Roundup | 75 | 700 | 690 | 1,465 | 5-7 min |
| Breaking News | 50 | 300 | 200 | 550 | 2-3 min |
| Events & Culture | 150 | 900 | 1,450 | 2,500+ | 8-10 min |
| Community Spotlight | 100 | 700 | 1,000 | 1,800+ | 6-8 min |

### Tone Across All

- **Warm & personal**: Like talking to family, not corporate
- **Celebratory**: Highlight wins, culture, diaspora pride
- **Action-oriented**: Give readers something to do or feel
- **Diaspora-aware**: Always include diaspora angle; acknowledge time zones, geographic spread, subscription tiers

### Diaspora Personalization

**Geographic segments** (optional):
- **North America** (45% USA, 20% Canada): Heavy US/Canada diaspora event listings, repatriation info
- **UK** (15%): London-area events, UK-Grenada flights, UK diaspora organizations
- **Caribbean neighbors** (8%): Regional news, inter-island travel, regional events

**Subscription tiers**:
- **Free**: All core content, limited event access
- **Premium** ($4.99/mo): Early event registration, exclusive stories, premium live streams
- **Family** ($9.99/mo): Family-friendly content, multi-user access, kids corner

**Time zones**:
- Always include multiple timezone options for live events
- Optimal send times: Sundays 9 AM EST (reaches USA + Canada morning)
- Friday evenings (6 PM EST) for weekend event focus

### Call-to-Action Strategy

**Weekly Roundup**:
- 3-4 per issue (read story, register event, join community, donate/support)
- Main: "Read full story," event registration, community contribution

**Breaking News**:
- 2-3 action items max (what to do, how to help, get updates)
- Primary: Emergency actions, help channels, live updates link

**Events & Culture**:
- 4-5 per issue (register, travel, watch, learn, participate)
- Primary: Event registration, travel packages, watch party RSVP

**Community Spotlight**:
- 4-5 per issue (donate, volunteer, nominate, connect, read more)
- Primary: Support the featured initiative, nominate next person, join community

## Assembly Workflow

### Step 1: Choose Template & Time

Decide which newsletter type + frequency:
- **Weekly Roundup**: Sunday mornings (3-4 hrs to curate)
- **Breaking News**: As events occur (30-60 min to write)
- **Events & Culture**: Bi-weekly Wednesdays (2-3 hrs to curate)
- **Community Spotlight**: Bi-weekly Mondays (2-3 hrs interviews + writing)

### Step 2: Gather Content

**Weekly Roundup**:
- Scan top island news (Grenada Broadcast Services, Loop Caribbean, local news)
- Pull upcoming events (check calendars)
- Identify diaspora angle for top story
- Find cultural/community spotlights (social, tips, interviews)

**Breaking News**:
- Verify facts from 2+ sources
- Get official statements
- Identify diaspora impact
- Gather emergency contacts

**Events & Culture**:
- Pull theme of month (rotation)
- Find 1 featured event + 10-15 supporting events
- Write cultural deep-dive (or pull from archives)
- Feature diaspora tradition

**Community Spotlight**:
- Interview featured person (1-2 hrs)
- Gather secondary stories (social, submissions)
- Collect community news
- Update organization directory

### Step 3: Write & Format

Use template structure:
- Replace placeholders with actual content
- Maintain tone (warm, personal, celebratory)
- Ensure all links work
- Add images (optimize for email clients)
- Test on mobile

### Step 4: Personalization

Prepare versions for:
- Premium subscribers (exclusive additions)
- Family tier (family-friendly focus)
- Geographic segments (optional; local events prioritized)

### Step 5: Send & Track

- Send at optimal time (9 AM EST Sundays for weekly)
- Monitor open rates, click rates, unsubscribes
- A/B test subject lines
- Collect feedback for next issue

## Quality Checklist (All Templates)

Before sending any newsletter:

**Content**:
- [ ] Facts verified from 2+ sources (where applicable)
- [ ] All names, titles, organizations spelled correctly
- [ ] Diaspora angle present and clear
- [ ] Tone is warm, personal, celebratory (not corporate)
- [ ] Word count within target range
- [ ] No hardcoded secrets or internal URLs

**Technical**:
- [ ] All links tested and working
- [ ] Mobile-friendly layout verified (stacked on small screens)
- [ ] Images optimized for email clients (< 1MB each)
- [ ] Images have alt-text for accessibility
- [ ] Subject line is clear and specific (no clickbait)
- [ ] Unsubscribe link prominently placed and functional

**Design**:
- [ ] High contrast (white background, dark text)
- [ ] Typography readable (sans-serif, 14-16px body)
- [ ] Spacing clear (sections visually separated)
- [ ] Emojis used sparingly (only section headers)
- [ ] CTA buttons clear and clickable
- [ ] Footer with unsubscribe + preferences + social

**Engagement**:
- [ ] 3-5 CTAs per issue (not too many)
- [ ] CTAs aligned with reader value (not just promotions)
- [ ] Personalization applied (tier-specific, geographic, timezone)
- [ ] Feedback loop clear (how to nominate, submit, suggest)
