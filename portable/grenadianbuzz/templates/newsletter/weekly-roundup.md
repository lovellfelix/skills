# GrenadianBuzz Weekly Roundup Template

**Purpose**: Digest-style newsletter covering top stories, cultural highlights, and community updates for diaspora readers

**Frequency**: Weekly (Sundays, 9 AM EST)  
**Audience**: All subscribers (free, premium, family tiers)  
**Time to Read**: 5-7 minutes

## Personalization Tips

**For Premium Subscribers**:
- Add exclusive content previews
- Early access to event registration
- Bonus: 1-2 exclusive stories or cultural pieces

**For Family Tier**:
- Highlight family-friendly events
- Include a "Kids Corner" with educational content about Grenada
- Promote multi-user features (shared reading lists)

**Geographic Segments** (optional):
- **North American focus** (45% USA, 20% Canada): Heavy US/Canada diaspora event listings
- **UK focus** (15%): London-area community events, UK-Grenada flights
- **Caribbean neighbors** (8%): Regional news, travel between islands
