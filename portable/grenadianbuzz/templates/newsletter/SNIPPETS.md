## Subject Line Best Practices

### ✅ DO

- **Personalize**: Use `[NAME]`, `[CITY]`, `[PREFERENCE]` tags for segmentation
- **Add emojis sparingly**: 1-2 cultural emoji (🇬🇩 🎭 🎶 🌴 💚) resonate with diaspora
- **Be specific**: "[X] things" or event name beats generic "your update"
- **Match tone to audience**: Warm for community, urgent for alerts, festive for culture
- **Test variants**: A/B test top 3 subject lines by open rate

### ❌ DON'T

- **All caps** (except alerts): `URGENT` is OK, `YOUR WEEKLY ROUNDUP` is not
- **Misleading claims**: "You won't believe" or clickbait hurt trust
- **Vague references**: Avoid "Big news" or "You'll want to see this"
- **Overstuff emojis**: Looks unprofessional, hard to read
- **Forget timezone context**: Diaspora readers span 5+ timezones

---

## CTA Best Practices

### ✅ DO

- **Action verb + direction**: "Read more →", "Donate now →"
- **Create urgency** (when appropriate): "Only 2 spots left", "Closes [DATE]"
- **Match button style**: Primary action gets prominent button; secondary uses text link
- **Segment CTAs**: Different audiences may want different actions
- **Test placement**: Top CTA vs. mid-content vs. footer

### ❌ DON'T

- **Generic "Click here"**: Doesn't tell user what they'll get
- **Too many CTAs**: Max 3-5 per newsletter, focus top 1-2
- **Hidden links**: Make CTAs obvious and clickable
- **Passive voice**: "Learn more" > "Information is available"
- **Ignore mobile**: Ensure buttons/links are thumb-friendly (44px minimum)
