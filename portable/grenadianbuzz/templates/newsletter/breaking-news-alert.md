# GrenadianBuzz Breaking News Alert Template

**Purpose**: Real-time, urgent notification for major news events affecting diaspora or island

**Frequency**: As needed (not scheduled; typically 2-5 per month)  
**Audience**: All subscribers (includes opted-in to breaking news alerts)  
**Time to Read**: 2-3 minutes  
**Send Method**: Email + SMS (premium feature)

### 2. Headline & Lead (5-10 sentences, max)

**Essential info** (Inverted pyramid style):
- **What**: Concise, specific event
- **When**: Date, time, timezone
- **Where**: Island, parish, or diaspora location
- **Who**: Key figures, agencies, impact scope
- **Why it matters**: Diaspora angle, significance

**Example**:

```
Government Declares State of Emergency After Flash Flooding in St. John's

Heavy rainfall over the past 6 hours has caused flooding in multiple 
parishes. The government declared a state of emergency at 2 PM EST.

Latest: 500+ people evacuated | 3 schools closed | No deaths reported

Impact on diaspora: Flights from Barbados being diverted; repatriation 
efforts underway if needed.
```

## Event Categories & Templates

### 🌪️ Natural Disasters (Hurricanes, Flooding, Earthquakes)

**Key additions**:
- Weather data (wind speed, rainfall, atmospheric pressure)
- NMS (National Meteorological Services) forecast
- Building damage reports (if available)
- Evacuation zone map links
- How to help section (mandatory)

### 🏛️ Government / Political (Elections, Policy Changes, Scandals)

**Key additions**:
- Official statement from relevant ministry/office
- Political party responses (if multi-party issue)
- Diaspora implications (visa policy, investment, etc.)
- Historical context (previous similar events)
- "What this means for you" explainer

### 🚑 Health Emergencies (Disease Outbreaks, Major Accidents)

**Key additions**:
- Ministry of Health statement
- Symptoms or safety guidelines (if disease)
- Testing/vaccination centers (if applicable)
- Public health authority hotline
- Travel impact (if quarantine required)

### ⚽ Sports / Cultural Events (Major wins, cancellations)

**Key additions**:
- Score/results
- Diaspora fan reaction
- Upcoming fixtures/events
- Where to watch

### 💼 Economic / Business News (Major layoffs, business launches, currency changes)

**Key additions**:
- Ministry of Finance statement
- Expert analysis
- Impact on diaspora (remittances, investments, etc.)
- What to do if affected

## Writing Guidelines

### Tone
- **Calm & factual**: Avoid sensationalism or ALL CAPS
- **Empathetic**: Acknowledge impact on people, families
- **Action-oriented**: Tell readers what to do
- **Diaspora-aware**: Always include diaspora angle

### Accuracy Checklist
- [ ] All facts verified from 2+ sources
- [ ] Names, titles, agencies spelled correctly
- [ ] Numbers (casualties, evacuations) current as of send time
- [ ] Quotes attributed and verified
- [ ] No speculation presented as fact ("may," "could" used carefully)
- [ ] Timestamp included (when last updated)

### Accessibility
- [ ] Read aloud for clarity (no jargon)
- [ ] Hyperlinks descriptive (not "click here")
- [ ] Images have alt-text
- [ ] High contrast (white background, dark text)
- [ ] Mobile-friendly layout

## Send Protocol

| Event Severity | Send Immediately | Update Frequency | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical** (hurricanes, major accidents, deaths) | Yes | Every 30 min | 24-48 hrs |
| **Major** (flooding, power outages, political events) | Yes | Every 1-2 hrs | 12-24 hrs |
| **Significant** (school closures, traffic disruptions) | Yes | Every 2-4 hrs | 6-12 hrs |
| **Minor** (local events, small incidents) | No (digest) | N/A | N/A |

**Critical events**: May send SMS alerts to premium subscribers in parallel
